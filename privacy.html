Team Shuffle

Privacy Policy

For the Team Shuffle Android app and web app · Effective 14 September 2026

In short: Team Shuffle does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The names and lists you type are stored only on your own device, and you can delete them at any time.

Who we are

Team Shuffle is a random team and pair generator made by an independent developer — the developer shown on the app’s Google Play listing (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Team Shuffle Android app and the web version at pscdrac-creater.github.io/team-shuffle.

For any privacy question or request, email piyushc3@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your device

To do its job, Team Shuffle keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the names you add to the current list;
  • lists you save, and the names you give them;
  • your split settings (team size or number of teams).

This information never leaves your device through the app. We have no servers, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

Information we collect

None. Team Shuffle has no accounts or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting tools and no third-party tracking code. The app does not send the names you enter, or any other information, to us or to anyone else.

Copying and sharing results

When you tap Copy (or Share, where your browser offers it), the team list goes to your device’s clipboard or to the app you pick. That only happens when you choose it, and from then on the privacy policy of the app you paste or share into applies.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include Team Shuffle’s stored lists in your device backup, which Google keeps in your Google Account under Google’s Privacy Policy. We never receive and cannot access these backups.

Permissions

The Android app does not ask for any runtime permissions — no contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Its app framework declares Android’s standard internet permission, but Team Shuffle does not use it to send any data. Tapping “Privacy policy” in the app opens this page in your web browser.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don’t receive that information. The web version keeps your lists in your browser’s storage on your device, just like the app.

How your information is protected

Your lists stay on your device, where Android keeps each app’s storage private to that app. Because Team Shuffle never uploads them, there is no copy on any server that could be exposed.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • remove a name by tapping it, or tap Clear all to empty the current list;
  • delete a saved list by tapping ✕ on it;
  • erase everything at once in Android Settings (Apps › Team Shuffle › Storage › Clear storage; the wording varies a little between phones), or by uninstalling the app. On the web, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side — but you are always welcome to contact us with a request.

Children

Team Shuffle does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Team Shuffle ever begins collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that version of the app is released.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: piyushc3@gmail.com
